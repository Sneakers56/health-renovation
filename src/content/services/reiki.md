Reiki is a Japanese technique for stress reduction and relaxation that also promotes healing. It is based on the principle that life force energy ("ki") flows through us, and that when this energy is low or blocked, we are more susceptible to illness and stress.

During a Reiki session, Mark uses gentle, light touch (or near-the-body hand positions) to channel healing energy into the body's energy centres. Sessions are deeply relaxing and profoundly beneficial for both physical and emotional wellbeing.

Reiki is effective for stress, anxiety, pain, grief, recovery from illness, and general wellbeing. It works beautifully alongside conventional medical treatment and other therapies.
