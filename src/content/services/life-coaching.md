Life coaching with Mark offers a structured, supportive space to explore what you truly want from life – and to build the clarity, confidence, and practical strategies to move towards it.

Unlike therapy, life coaching is forward-focused. It's not about analysing the past, but about understanding where you are now, identifying where you want to be, and creating a clear roadmap to get there. Mark brings his deep understanding of psychology, energy, and human behaviour to every coaching session.

Whether you're at a crossroads in life, feeling stuck in old patterns, or simply want more from your relationships, work, or health – coaching can be the catalyst for real, lasting change.
